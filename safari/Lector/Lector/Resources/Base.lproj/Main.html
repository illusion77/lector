Lector

Read any page aloud, one sentence at a time.

  1. 1
    Turn it on Open Settings › Apps › Safari › Extensions › Lector. Turn it on and allow it on All Websites.
  2. 2
    Open it on a page In Safari, tap the page menu in the address bar, then Lector. The player appears at the bottom.
  3. 3
    Listen Tap play. Skip back and forward a sentence at a time, or select any text to start reading from it.

The voice runs on your iPhone. Nothing you read leaves your device.
